Company

We are building the system that makes every commercial decision in an industrial company evidence based.

Large companies spend millions on pricing and growth programs that end with a report. Aristotle turns that work into software that runs every day, so mid market industrial companies get the same capability at a fraction of the cost.

Why now

Three shifts make AI-native pricing and growth possible today.

payments
Pricing is the strongest profit lever

A one percent price improvement lifts operating profit far more than the same gain in volume or cost, yet most mid market companies still price from spreadsheets.

cloud_sync
Commercial data now sits in one place

Cloud ERP and CRM systems hold quotes, orders and accounts in a form that software can read directly.

smart_toy
Agents can do the analyst work

Language model agents now read filings, quotes and market data, and check their own work, at a cost no consulting team can match.

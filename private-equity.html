Portfolio rollout

One data model per portfolio company, the same specialist team, and impact measured against a baseline agreed with each CFO. Results and methods carry from one company to the next.

How it works arrow_forward

What a portfolio company gives

One analyst, a few hours a week, and read-only extracts.

Days 0 to 30

Diagnostic on the company's own data. Opportunity sized in margin and revenue.

Days 31 to 90

First price actions and account plans live, each signed off by a specialist and the company's owner.

Every quarter

Models retrain on results, the next tranche is sized, impact reviewed with finance.

The data model, the models and the applications stay with the company.

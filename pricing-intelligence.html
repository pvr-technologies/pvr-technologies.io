Pricing Intelligence

Finds margin leakage in every quote and recommends the right price for each customer and product.

Pricing Intelligence reads quotes, orders and price books, segments customers and products, and shows where prices fall below target. Pricing teams ask questions in plain English and receive tables, charts, a deck and a workbook they can take straight to a pricing committee.

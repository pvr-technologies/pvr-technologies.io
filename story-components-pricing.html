The challenge

The company had no single system of record for pricing. Price books were updated by hand, and the commercial team could not see how quote prices compared with targets across customers and products.

What was done

  • Built customer and product segmentation across business units.
  • Found pricing discrepancies at quote level and sized the margin at stake.
  • Developed price and volume curves for the top products to set price corridors.
  • Designed the process and tooling for a regular price book refresh.

The result

Pricing Intelligence found $8M of margin and gave the pricing team a repeatable way to refresh prices. The client reports a return of 12 times the cost of the deployment in year one, and rising.
